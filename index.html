How it works

Three simple principles, applied to every word

Aa

A real dictionary

Suggestions are built on Lexique 4 (New, Pallier, Schalchli, Bourgin & Gimenes), a scientifically validated French lexicon, not a guessed word list.

Gr

Grammatically aware

Agreement, conjugation, and elisions (l', d', qu'...) are handled automatically, so it never suggests a word that sounds wrong in the sentence.

🔒

100% local

Nothing you type is ever sent to a server. The dictionary and your settings stay in your browser.

Privacy

What the extension does, and doesn't do

What's stored: your settings, your personal dictionary, and your list of banned words are saved locally in your browser (and synced via your Google account if Chrome sync is on).

What's never collected: the text you write is never saved, transmitted, or analyzed on a server. The extension only works with what's already in your browser.

Dictionary updates: the extension periodically checks whether a new version of the linguistic dictionary is available, and downloads it automatically: that's the only network connection it ever makes.
